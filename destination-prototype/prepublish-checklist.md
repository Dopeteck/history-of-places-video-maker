# Destination-history video pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Human reviewer approved the exact script and its content hash.
- [ ] Every factual claim is supported by one of the listed primary or authoritative sources; unsupported claims were removed.
- [ ] 45–60 second narration was synthesized with Kokoro and scene cuts use measured speech timings.
- [ ] Every asset appears in `assets_manifest.json` with source URL, author, exact license/version, retrieval date, local filename, scene ID, and dimensions.
- [ ] Commercial-use license passed the allowlist; CC BY / CC BY-SA attribution is present in `description.txt`.
- [ ] Faces, logos/brands, and modern buildings/artworks with unclear reuse rights are flagged for human review.
- [ ] All text is rendered as real typography; phone safe areas, title, labels, and highlighted captions are legible and unclipped.
- [ ] Only one photographic visual style is used; no stickmen or cartoon characters.
- [ ] Map geography, pins, and route are checked against authoritative locations.
- [ ] Sources and asset credits are present in the description.
- [ ] AI/synthetic narration disclosure reminder completed; set YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure when applicable.
- [ ] At least one extracted frame per scene reviewed against the MP4.
- [ ] No publishing or Telegram delivery before explicit user approval.
- [ ] Current prices, opening hours, and entry rules are omitted unless dated and sourced; otherwise show “Check current details before visiting.”
